How to play

Two players take turns moving one piece at a time. White moves first. Capture by landing on an opponent's piece. The goal is to checkmate the opposing king.

Click a piece, then click a highlighted square to move. Click another of your own pieces to change selection.

Pieces

  • ♔King — one square in any direction. Cannot move into check.
  • ♕Queen — any number of squares, straight or diagonal.
  • ♖Rook — any number of squares, straight only.
  • ♗Bishop — any number of squares, diagonally.
  • ♘Knight — L-shape (two then one). Jumps over pieces.
  • ♙Pawn — forward 1 (or 2 from its start row). Captures diagonally.

Special moves

  • ⇄Castling — king moves two squares toward a rook; the rook jumps over to the king's other side. Only if neither has moved, no pieces between, and the king is not in / passing through check.
  • ↗En passant — if an enemy pawn moves two squares forward beside yours, you may capture it on the next move only, as if it had moved one.
  • ★Promotion — a pawn reaching the last rank promotes to queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

Ending the game

  • +Check — your king is attacked. You must respond by moving the king, blocking, or capturing the attacker.
  • #Checkmate — in check with no legal escape. The other player wins.
  • =Stalemate — no legal move but not in check. The game is a draw.
